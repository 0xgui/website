## Open source & robotics

### SO-101 arms + LeRobot

[github.com/0xgui/SO-101](https://github.com/0xgui/SO-101)

Built leader and follower arms, recorded **50** teleoperated episodes (**23,733** frames) with LeRobot, then trained an ACT policy on them and ran it on the arm. Dataset [0xGUI/so101_first_motion](https://huggingface.co/datasets/0xGUI/so101_first_motion) and policy [0xGUI/act_so101_first_motion](https://huggingface.co/0xGUI/act_so101_first_motion) are on the Hub.
